ONE-TO-ONE TRAINING

A PROGRAMME WRITTEN FOR YOU.

Remote, wherever you are.

We start with an assessment: we work out where you are starting from, what you can do today, and where you want to get to. My MSc in Preventive and Adapted Physical Activity is built for adapted physical activity, which means training safely anyone who starts from a particular situation (a health condition, an injury, years without moving) or who simply wants to get back in shape.

I do not do rehabilitation and I do not treat medical conditions. If you need clinical support, I will tell you and point you in the right direction.
